

# Challenge: Combine Two Lists Into a Map

## Scenario

You have two lists; one containing country names and another containing their capitals.  
Your goal is to combine these lists into a single map where **each country** is the key and its **capital** is the value.

Your Task
	1.	Use Terraform functions to combine the two lists into a map.
	2.	Assign the result to a new local variable called country_capitals.

Bonus Challenge 1
Try changing the order of the lists or their lengths, what happens?
